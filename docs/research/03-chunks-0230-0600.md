## Takeaways for StageDeck
- Everything on the wall in this range is either (a) quick opacity/blur state changes with **no positional motion**
  (UI mocks, cards, slide changes ≈0.2s) or (b) title effects built on blur, chromatic split and right→left letter
  dissolves. No springy overshoot anywhere in this range.
- UI-mock builds are far faster than our `rise` (≈0.15–0.2s vs ≈1s settle) — use fade/blur only inside mocks.
